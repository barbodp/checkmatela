Start with your event.

Fit and timing

Before you decide.

  1. Choose the dress code

    Black tie usually points toward a tuxedo. Weddings and family events allow more flexibility.

  2. Check fit

    Measure chest, waist, height and inseam, then compare with the guide and ask about the garment.

  3. Check your date

    Build in time to try on the complete look and adjust it if needed.
