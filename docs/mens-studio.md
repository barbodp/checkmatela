# Men's studio photo production

Production uses the built-in image-generation tool. Models are original fictional adults, with a different identity for every product. Each product retains its assigned model across all colors and camera angles. Studio: white seamless background, diffuse light, charcoal trousers, head-to-upper-thigh portrait.

## Common prompt
Use case: product-mockup / photorealistic ecommerce fashion catalog. Create ONE photograph of ONE original fictional handsome adult male model wearing the exact garment in the garment reference. Match the established CheckmateLA studio: pure white seamless background, bright soft diffuse even lighting, realistic skin and precise fabric texture, no marble, props, furniture, text, watermark, jewelry, tattoos or accessories. Portrait 2:3 composition, entire head through upper thighs, complete garment hem and cuffs visible, arms and hands naturally down and clear of garment, tailored dark charcoal trousers. Garment must match its source photo exactly in color, pattern placement and scale, texture, material, collar, fastening, pockets, seams, trim, sleeve length, cuffs, lining and silhouette. Remove hangtags/mannequins. Garment source is authoritative over color names. Preserve small construction details; do not import clothing construction from pose references. If a reference contains a supplier human, use ONLY their garment, never copy their face, hair, identity, pose or trousers. No multi-angle collage or split views. This is a single camera exposure.

## Camera prompts
### front
Single straight-on FRONT photograph, shoulders level, face and torso facing camera, relaxed poised friendly neutral expression.

### threequarter
Single THREE-QUARTER photograph, body rotated about 40 degrees, face naturally toward camera. Show garment side construction; both hands down and unobstructing.

### back
Single exact straight-on BACK photograph. Body faces directly away, both shoulders square and parallel to the camera plane, camera perpendicular to back. Back of head only, NO face, cheek or profile. One centered torso and one pair of arms, no collage, no inset or second angle.

## Reference roles and fidelity
Initial models are generated from physical descriptions. Flat garment photos supply clothing only. Where a supplier photo contains a human model, create the fictional identity independently first, then use that identity as reference 1 and the garment photo as reference 2; ignore the supplier person's identity. Use supplied actual rear product photos wherever available. Color names never override source pixels. Keep every color gallery separate, with front, three-quarter, and single straight-on back before original product photos. Jewelry, labels and hangtags are excluded. No marble or multi-angle composites.

AF-1205 elbow patches: use actual rear reference for both the back and three-quarter patch construction. Patches are dark coffee-brown matte faux suede, tall rounded rectangles with straight sides and rounded corners, not ovals. Preserve them across every body color.
