Shipping & Returns

Free white-glove shipping over $500, and easy returns if it isn't right.

Delivery

Shipping.

01

Free over $500

White-glove shipping is complimentary on every order over $500.

02

Standard delivery

Orders under $500 ship for a flat rate, calculated at checkout.

03

Timing

Ready-to-wear ships within 2–3 business days. Made-to-order pieces may take longer — we'll confirm before you pay.

Returns

Returns & exchanges.

  1. Within 30 days

    Unworn garments with tags attached can be returned for a refund or exchange within 30 days of delivery.

  2. Contact us

    Email suit.shop.dtla@gmail.com with your order details and we'll send instructions.

  3. Send it back

    Pack it in the original garment bag and box. Return shipping is free on exchanges.

  4. Refund

    Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days of receipt.

FAQ

Good to know.

Can altered garments be returned?

Altered garments are made specifically for you, so they can't be returned — but we'll adjust them again until they're right.

Do you ship internationally?

We're focused on the United States for now. Email us for other destinations.

What if it arrives damaged?

Contact us within 48 hours with photos and we'll make it right.
