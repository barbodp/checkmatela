Shipping & Returns

Clear terms on shipping, returns, exchanges and alterations — so you can order with confidence.

Delivery

Shipping.

Standard shipping is $18 and free on orders over $500. Express shipping is $35. Local pickup in Los Angeles is free. Exact delivery windows are confirmed at checkout based on your address and the items in your order.

Returns

Return & exchange windows.

Applies to unworn items in original condition — see the checklist below
CategoryWindow from delivery
Suits, tuxedos & individual pieces (King, Pawn, Queen)14 days
Accessories & shoes (Bishop, Rook)30 days
How it works

Exchanges and refunds.

01

Free exchange

Swap for a different size, color or style once per item, at no cost — we cover the shipping both ways.

02

$12 refund fee

Choosing a refund instead of an exchange deducts a flat $12 return-shipping fee from the amount refunded. A second exchange on an item that's already used its free one is treated the same way.

03

Our mistake, no fee

If an item arrives damaged, defective or wrong, return shipping is free and you receive a full refund or replacement. The $12 fee never applies here.

Fit

Alterations, included.

Every suit and dress purchase includes one complimentary round of alterations, through our Los Angeles studio or a Book a Fitting appointment. Additional alterations after that first round are billed at cost.

Condition checklist

What qualifies for a return.

01

Unworn & unwashed

Tags still attached, with no signs of wear.

02

No odor or marks

Free of deodorant, cologne, perfume or makeup marks.

03

Vents & pockets closed

On suits and jackets, vents and pockets must still be stitched or unopened.

04

Shoes indoors only

Soles unmarked, tried on indoors, original box included.

05

Unaltered

Not hemmed, taken in or otherwise tailored.

Exceptions

Not returnable.

Monogrammed or personalized items and anything marked final sale or clearance cannot be returned or exchanged.

Ordering as a group

Group & groomsmen orders.

Wedding parties and other group orders follow the same terms above, item by item. Because a wave of returns close to an event date is the hardest to handle in time, we recommend confirming every size through Book a Fitting before you order.

How to start

Starting a return or exchange.

Online checkout is not yet connected, so returns, exchanges and alterations appointments are handled directly: email suit.shop.dtla@gmail.com or use Book a Fitting with your order details. Once an item is received, refunds are processed within 5 business days.
