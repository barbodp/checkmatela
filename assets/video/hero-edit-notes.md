# Category hero film direction

The men's film now uses its own seamless white cyclorama and a black-veined marble slab. The original lead model reference is saved at `assets/img/hero-video/men-model-reference.webp`; use it to preserve his identity in future men's edits. Other categories retain their existing warm ivory studio and quiet editorial pace.

- **Men:** six ordered shots of the same handsome model in a charcoal three-piece suit: full outfit walking toward camera and buttoning the jacket; lapels and tie; trousers and shoes taking two slow steps; black belt and brass buckle; full back walking toward the marble; seated on the marble with a calm gaze. Each shot is a separate Higgsfield image-to-video generation from a matching reference frame. The edit uses restrained hard cuts and muted slow-motion pacing.
- **Women:** seated full gown, standing full gown, then draped fabric detail.
- **Kids:** standing full suit, jacket and tie, then shoes.
- **Accessories:** equal four-second views of cufflinks/pocket square/bow tie, men's and women's belts, then women's earrings/bracelet/silk. The three product stills used for this edit are in `assets/img/hero-video/`. No purse or handbag.
- **Shoes:** four equal three-second walking shots. Desktop and mobile use the same cut points and pace.

All films are muted, H.264, 24 fps. Desktop films are 1280 × 720; the shoe mobile film is 720 × 1280. Each page uses a matching still poster for reduced motion and loading fallback.
